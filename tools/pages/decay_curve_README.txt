DECAY curve. You said on the module: "the decay seems to bias towards shorter decays until noon, then 12-3 controls longer decays, which is a small zone to dial in longer decays with precision ... going past 3 o'clock shifts into endless tails, but is there any granularity in effect past 3?" There isn't today: from about half past 3 to fully right, the knob does nothing new. That's an hour and a half of the knob.

These versions give that travel back to the long tails. Every version keeps each SPACE's shortest and longest tail (HALL 0.8 to 60 s), and endless still sounds and behaves the same once you're there: it follows your playing, then holds. Only where things sit on the knob changes. The table at the top shows the decay time at each hour; it's the main thing to compare.

A_today       Today. Finite to 3 o'clock, endless from about half past 3.
B_stretched   Today's curve stretched out. Endless from half past 4 (the last half hour of the knob). Everything comes later, so noon is shorter than today (HALL 8 s, not 12).
C_bent        B, bent so noon stays where it is today (HALL 12 s), and the long tails get the extra knob. HALL's 15 to 60 s gets 110 degrees of the knob, against 77 today.
D_late        C's bend, with endless only in the last sliver (from about 10 to 5). The most knob for long tails (HALL's 15 to 60 s gets 119 degrees), the smallest endless zone.

The cards: HALL (SIZE full, so the times are the table's) and CANYON (SIZE noon), each at 9, 11, noon, 1, 2, 3 and 4 o'clock and fully right. The fully-right cards (8 and 16) are the same in every version, to the bit: endless is endless; they're there to compare against 4 o'clock. Each card has the pad, the stabs and the piano: 7 seconds of playing, then 18 seconds of tail. Cards 17 to 19 are a slow DECAY sweep for PLATE, HALL and CANYON (SIZE noon), and card 20 is CANYON's sweep again at full SIZE, where SIZE sets the shortest tail to 12 s and DECAY spreads from there to 120 s (the table's "CANYON S1" rows): from 7 o'clock to fully right over 30 seconds, a chord every 4 seconds, then 12 seconds held at fully right. Each hour of the knob is 3 seconds: noon at 0:17, 3 o'clock at 0:26, 4 o'clock at 0:29, fully right at 0:32.

These are not level-matched. How long the tail lasts is the question, and a longer tail is also a fuller one, so a version can sound louder where its tail is longer. The renders are 100 % wet; the MIX fader blends the dry back in.

What to listen for: at 1, 2 and 3 o'clock, whether each step of the knob is a useful step in length. At 4 o'clock, whether you'd rather have a long tail there (B, C, D) or endless (A). On the sweeps, whether the approach to endless feels like a smooth arrival or a switch. Pick the one you'd keep.
