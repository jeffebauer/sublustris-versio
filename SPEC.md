# SUBLUSTRIS VERSIO — Firmware Spec

**Name:** Sublustris Versio (Latin *sublustris*, "dimly lit, faintly glowing": the half-light of a clouded night). Firmware target name `sublustris_versio`. Sibling to Resilio Versio. Renamed from Immensio Versio (Latin *immensus*, "boundless"), 27 Oct 2026, ADR 0032; the repo, the internal identifiers (`iv::`, `IV_*`) and the plugin's codes keep the old name.
**Target:** Noise Engineering Versio platform (Electro-Smith Daisy Seed inside).
**Goal:** a huge, warm, lush, characterful reverb for dub techno, ambient and dark ambient. Supermassive / Cathedra scale and drift, played like an instrument. Never metallic.
**Status:** Spec **v1.0 (frozen)**, 21 Oct 2026. Input: `IMMENSIO_BRIEF.md`. Vocabulary: `CONTEXT.md`. Decisions: `docs/adr/` (0001–0017; index in `docs/adr/README.md`). Session record: `docs/spec-session.md`. Changes after freeze: new ADR + changelog entry. Numbers marked *(start)* are starting guesses; tuned numbers replace them as milestones confirm them.

## 2. Sound target

### 2.1 Core characteristics
- **Warm and lush, never metallic.** Reference dislikes: Make Noise Erbe-Verb, Plexophone.
- **Sustains near-infinitely without running away.** At infinite, the tail evolves to a warm floor and holds there (ADR 0008).
- **Massive mode has mass:** a gentle built-in swell in NORMAL (or a timed BLOOM) that starts with the source, and SIZE from a long wash to a boundless one (§4.5, §4.11; v1.0.6).
- **WEAR is one wear amount** (ADR 0027): tape saturation, a BBD's breathing and bit and rate grit deepen together over the Neve's warmth, without darkening the tail or moving its pitch (§4.7). *(v1.0.60, ADR 0036: the radio's band-pass moved to MOTION's right side; v1.0.42; v1.0.30-v1.0.41 ERA ran from sweet to gritty through five eras, ADR 0021; before that a stacked journey back in time, ADR 0003.)*
- **Its own instrument:** not a Dattorro plate with distortion (Plateau Versio), and no Desmodus-style "scream".

### 2.2 Inspirations → what we take
Valhalla Supermassive (Sagittarius, Andromeda, Large Magellanic Cloud: swell, mass, echoes → wash), Meris Mercury7 Cathedra (in-tank pitch drift), Eventide Blackhole (shaping the envelope), Vongon Ultrasheer (vintage bits, warble), Lexicon 224 / Griesinger (modulated delays against metallic ringing), Alesis Midiverb (12-bit, ~10 kHz, ~23.4 kHz rate), the dub mixing desk send/return (EQ and drive in the loop, played like an instrument: Tone and the saturation), Neve (even-harmonic warmth), Portastudio (compression, head bump, hiss, wobble). Details and sources: `IMMENSIO_BRIEF.md` §2 and `docs/spec-session.md` "[VERIFY] results".

### 2.3 Design principles
1. Every tank delay is modulated (the anti-metallic floor), delay lengths are mutually prime, and damping sits in the loop.
2. In-loop gain is ≤ 1 at every frequency for every setting. Infinite means exactly 1 at the floor, held there by the soft saturation. No setting adds energy.
3. Bit loss truncates toward zero, so a fading tail always reaches silence or its floor and never holds a stuck buzz (a limit cycle). This follows Resilio's `EchoBits`.
4. Every control move is smooth: no zipper, no clicks. Size moves bend in pitch on purpose (ADR 0012); nothing else does.
5. Sound work happens on the desktop first (Renderer, then Plugin). Hardware is for profiling and final feel.
6. Deterministic: every random source is seeded, so the same input and params give bit-identical output.

### Button + Gate (one gesture reader, ADR 0009–0011)
The button reads **FREEZE / PAINT** on the panel (tap / hold) and the gate **FREEZE** (ADR 0029).
- **Tap** (press < 0.3 s) **latches the freeze.** The big tank becomes a still pad (ADR 0009); the input moves to the **twin**, a quieter layer over the pad that fades back out (Size and Decay play it; v1.0.7). In HALL and MASSIVE the twin is a small room about 8 dB under the pad, following its level (v1.0.43, ADR 0028); in PLATE it is a second Plate. Tap again to unfreeze: the pad decays naturally, and the big tank glides to the current Size and Decay over ~1 s (ADR 0009).
- **Timing:** freeze engages **on the press** (no latency, so you catch the chord). While frozen, a press opens a 0.3 s window. Released inside it: unfreeze on release. Still held at 0.3 s: **paint**.
- **Hold to paint** (while frozen): a little of the twin or room (what is playing) blends into the pad over ~3 s *(start)*, at most ~1.5 dB, so the pad stays itself; on release the pad keeps the new layers. With no hold, the pad is untouched (v1.0.7).
- **Gate = the same gestures:** a rising edge is a press, a falling edge is a release. A short pulse toggles; a gate held over 0.3 s while frozen paints. Unpatched, the gate reads low and does nothing.
- Ramps *(start)*: input to the big tank closes over 20 ms on freeze; the in-loop colour glides out over 0.2 s; the twin's input opens over 20 ms.

### LEDs (ADR 0016)
Level meters as Resilio (ADR 0031): LED 1–2 In L / In R, LED 3–4 Out L / Out R, green → amber with level, red on clip or limiter. When frozen, the output pair turns cool blue under the meter; painting pulses it slowly (~1 Hz).

### 4.2 Plate engine
- Dattorro-family figure-8 tank: input diffusers, 2 cross-coupled branches of modulated allpasses and delays, damping, multi-tap stereo output. Written from the 1997 paper; **no Plateau Versio code** (it is GPL-3.0).
- What makes it ours: every delay is modulated (floor + MOTION), and the loop carries the character chain (TONE, saturation, the Neve, WEAR's compression).
- **Twin (v1.0.7, replaces the companion; the small room in HALL and MASSIVE since v1.0.43, ADR 0028):** while frozen, PLATE's input goes to a second Plate of the same code; Size and Decay play it, its T60 at most 8 s; it has the loop's Neve and MOTION's per-line movement. In HALL and MASSIVE it goes to **the small room** (`dsp/TwinRoom.h`, after Undulae Music's Tinyspace, ideas only): a 150 Hz Butterworth high-pass, two allpasses a side, eight short lines (8-50 ms at the longest, by SIZE) mixed by a Hadamard matrix, at 24 kHz inside a half-rate stage. Its loss is DECAY's T60 (at most 8 s), the highs' T60 TONE's share × 0.6 ("room softer", `small_room` E). Each line's read wanders by 0.5 % of its own length (v1.0.47), so the room bends what it holds by 6-7.5 cents p95 at any SIZE, as the main tank does. SIZE glides its lines (at most a semitone, no crossfade). Its level: at SIZE 0.9 with DECAY infinite −8.63 dB (HALL) / −7.65 dB (MASSIVE) before the twin level, about 8 dB under the pad; elsewhere it follows the pad as the second tank did (v1.0.43), trimmed −0.93 / −0.88 / −0.53 dB at SIZE 0 / 0.5 / 0.9 (v1.0.47). Its rings, ~42 KB, are in AXI SRAM. *(v1.0.7-v1.0.42 HALL and MASSIVE had a second FDN, with its own swell in MASSIVE, v1.0.10.)*

### 4.3 FDN engine (Hall and Massive)
- **8 lines** *(start; 16 is a stretch goal, decided at M3)*. Mixed feedforward + feedback as Costello describes. Householder or Hadamard feedback matrix (fast 8-point Walsh transform, 24 add/sub).
- Delay lengths are mutually prime, spread by a Warp-style ratio control (Size in Massive). Each line has a modulated read: allpass interpolation (v1.0.2), floor modulation plus MOTION's per-line share. HALL's floor is 3× MASSIVE's (v1.0.25) and deepens with its tail (v1.0.57, ADR 0035): as is up to a 5 s tail, rising in a straight line to twice that from 15 s and at endless, glided over ~1 s and centred on the lines' mean read, so a DECAY move doesn't retune a held note.
- **Still lows (v1.0.18, ADR 0012 amendment):** the tank's input splits at 300 Hz (Butterworth, power complementary: the two tanks' outputs are uncorrelated and add in power). The highs' tank runs at 24 kHz (v1.0.35, ADR 0026: halfbands either side, `dsp/HalfRate.h`), the small room too. The lows go to a second 8-line FDN at 12 kHz (÷4, anti-aliased by the split and a 2 kHz low-pass) with **no modulation**, the same SIZE, DECAY, TONE, the loop's Neve and WEAR's compression, and in-loop diffusion, and the main tank's governor (which hears both). So the bass never wavers while everything above moves (Schlecht: a modulated low end sounds detuned; Keith Barr's dual-band reverb, Eventide's H90 Hall: one reverb per band).
- Per-line loop gain is set from T60 and the line length (Jot), so Decay doesn't retune.
- **Size moves (v1.0.34, ADR 0025; was a slew-limited glide, which retuned the whole tail by the length ratio):** once SIZE has rested 50 ms, every line, early tap and in-loop allpass jumps to its new length and the old read fades out over 0.3 s (PASP: crossfade between the two delay settings rather than glide). Within the loop the gains go sin² / cos², each line's scaled by how well its two reads agree (Bristow-Johnson): they sum to one where the reads agree and keep their power where they don't. A small HALL's in-loop diffusion joining or leaving runs on its own read (new or old); one present on both sides crossfades its length and coefficient. The loss filter fades from the old length's to the new one's with the fade, and for one trip the new read is corrected by the tail's own decay over the length difference. The level that follows SIZE (√ of the mean line, and the trims) is on the tank's input, so what's already circulating comes out as it went in. The lines crossfade in four groups (lines g and g + 4), one group after the other (v1.0.39: two; v1.0.45: four, ADR 0025 amendments), so only a quarter of them read twice at once: the crossfade's cost is memory, every read twice. DEPTH's predelay crossfades the same way (equal power: it isn't in a loop).
- **Hall voicing:** mean delay 35–260 ms (v1.0.8, v1.0.17), moderate input diffusion, no built-in mass (BLOOM still applies), predelay 0–60 ms (DEPTH's predelay adds in NATURAL). Its loss shelf crosses at 6 kHz (v1.0.57, ADR 0035; MASSIVE and the still lows at 2.5 kHz), so a long HALL's upper mids ring nearer the body (15 s: 2 / 4 / 8 kHz at 0.91 / 0.73 / 0.48 of 1 kHz's T60, near Valhalla Room's).
- Stereo: L and R inject into different lines (true stereo in); the outputs are taken from opposite-signed line sums.

### 4.4 Infinite and the floor (ADR 0008)
- Decay 0.85–1: the loop gain reaches 1 at the floor level. TONE's share, the Neve, WEAR's compression and the saturation stay in the loop, so a fed tail keeps darkening towards its floor (WEAR's grain is on the wet since v1.0.42). The always-on soft saturator plus a slow RMS loop-level governor (control rate) hold the tail at a warm floor *(−21 dBFS RMS since v1.0.51, HALL −18 since v1.0.52, ADR 0033; was −24)* so it never turns to mud or falls silent. The governor comes in across DECAY's 0.80–0.85 crossover in HALL; in PLATE and MASSIVE it eases in from 0.70 (weight t³ to 0.85, v1.0.56), so turning towards endless glides instead of dropping at 0.80.
- **The governor's correction is a flat gain (v1.0.58, ADR 0008 amended):** its loss above the floor or a held tail's ceiling, and the hold's make-up below it, are the same at the highs as at the lows; TONE's ratio, the air and the infinite fade shape DECAY's own loss only. (Until v1.0.57 any loss from it switched the air on and its make-up lifted the lows alone, so a held tail drained its highs and piled up its lows for minutes.) The still lows take their leader's floor loss but not the hold's correction. The wet's input loses its DC below 5 Hz (one pole, outside the loop): at endless the loop's gain at DC is 1, so any DC coming in stayed.
- **A held endless tail (v1.0.58; the input quiet for 2 s at full endless DECAY, or from v1.0.61 once the hold below is in), PLATE and HALL:** its modulation (the floor, PLATE's chorus and long-line wander: what FREEZE glides out) glides out over 10 s, so held notes stay notes instead of smearing into a haze; MOTION's own movement stays. If what was played was bright, its top end also mellows, at most by about 24 dB above the loss shelf at as built's early pace (0.4 dB/s at first), most of it within ~2 minutes, then settles: in proportion to the playing's brightness (its mean frequency by its slope, followed over 1 s while playing and held while quiet), none up to 1.2 kHz (a soft chord), all from 6 kHz (a noise or cymbal wash). At TONE noon and below the infinite fade's own darkening is the larger and stands. New playing brings both back (the modulation over 1 s). MASSIVE keeps its slow wash and its own darkening (ADR 0022). Not while frozen.
- **Endless follows the playing (v1.0.61, ADR 0008 amended):** while you play, DECAY past 0.80 is DECAY 0.80: its loss, level make-up, long-tail trims, MASSIVE's high ratio and its governor (at the floor above; eased in from 0.70 in PLATE and MASSIVE), so turning into or out of endless doesn't move the level, quiet or hot, and a hot input is bounded as the longest finite tail is. Across the 0.80-0.85 crossover only the hold comes in. Once the tank's input is 30 dB under the tail for 0.25 s the tail's loss glides out over 0.5 s and it holds where it is (the input stays open); the floor lets go within 50 ms of the input going quiet and glides to a −6 dBFS safety ceiling with the hold, and the hold's ceiling (the tail's own settled level, above) stops any creep. Still notes start with the hold. New playing lets go at once. (Until v1.0.60 endless was governed to the floor whatever you played, so a hot tail stepped down into it and a quiet one up, and a duck, §4.9, held it down under the playing.)
- **Freeze is different (ADR 0009):** the pad holds still. Gain is exactly 1, input is closed, the in-loop colour is bypassed (glided over 0.2 s), and only MOTION's per-line movement remains (the modulation floor glides out too, v1.0.22). Its hold is a flat gain (v1.0.58), so a pad keeps its top end. MOTION's tape transport and the rest of the wet (§4.10) still act on a frozen pad.

### 4.5 Massive mode: mass, echoes → wash (ADR 0007)
- **Swell stage:** a feedforward stage between the input diffusion and the tank: 12 taps of one long delay per side (v1.0.35, ADR 0026; was 24), spread over 1.25 × A with rising gains (total energy 1), jittered and sign-scrambled per side (v1.0.1; was "4 long modulated delays and allpasses", which can't be steered to a chosen attack time). The energy reaches the loop gradually, so the attack is a slab, not a hit; the 10 → 90 % rise lands at A. Below A ≈ 40 ms it hands over to the direct path. **Shared by every space** (it is also BLOOM, §4.11).
- **ONSET (was ATTACK) governs the attack in every space (ADR 0015).** In Massive, NATURAL keeps a gentle built-in mass: A = A₀(Decay) · m(Size), A₀ 0.5 → 0.2 s across Decay (log), m 1 → 1.5 across Size *(start)*. BLOOM replaces it with DEPTH's swell, still × m(Size), so Size adds a little mass. Longer swells are BLOOM's, where DEPTH sets them.
- **Size (v1.0.6; was echoes → wash):** low Size gives shorter lines (mean 0.35 s, spread 0.4) with diffusion 0.45: already a wash, no rhythmic echoes (the owner, M4). Turning Size up lengthens and fans out the lines and raises the diffusion, input and in-loop (two allpasses per line, 24–91 ms, coefficient 0.62 × diffusion), into a boundless wash, with predelay up to 100 ms. **Early taps** feed the output (4 of 6 per line since v1.0.36) so the wet starts within ~0.35 s of the source at any Size and swells from there.
- **Andromeda-scale tails stay tame** through §4.4: in-loop damping, soft saturation and the floor governor.
- **Highs ring with the body (v1.0.31, ADR 0022):** at TONE noon MASSIVE's high end decays as slowly as its body (its own share, `kMassiveHighT60Ratio`, ×1 under the Neve), as in Supermassive; TONE and the Neve darken it, and infinite still darkens towards its floor.

### 4.6 Loop character chain (per pass)
Order inside each line or branch: modulated delay read (allpass-interpolated) → TONE's loss (a shelf crossing at 2.5 kHz, v1.0.15; HALL's at 6 kHz, v1.0.57) + fixed damping and air → soft saturation (cheap polynomial or rational soft clip, no tanh, no oversampling in the loop) → the Neve's even harmonics → WEAR's tape compression (a little more loss above −18 dBFS). (The ducking, on the wet, is gone since v1.0.61, §4.9.) The dub-desk inspiration lives on through TONE, the saturation and WEAR; there is no resonant Desk EQ in the loop (ADR 0015): TONE's resonance is on the wet (v1.0.44, ADR 0031).

### 4.7 WEAR (ADR 0027, 0005; was ERA, ADR 0021)
One wear amount over the Neve (v1.0.42; `map::eraMix`, `dsp/EraTexture.h`). Its chain also carries MOTION's radio band (v1.0.60, ADR 0036), set by MOTION's right side, not by WEAR. **WEAR 0 is the Neve alone**, the default. Every stage comes in together over the first twentieth of the knob (a smoothstep), then deepens with it. Nothing darkens the tail (TONE's job) or moves its pitch (MOTION's). *(v1.0.30-v1.0.41: five eras, each on its own, Neve → Midiverb → bucket-brigade → cassette → AM radio, ADR 0021; before that stacked, ADR 0003.)*

| Stage | What it does | Grows with WEAR | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neve, the base (always) | even harmonics into the loop's soft clip; the tank's highs a little shorter (×0.7 T60 share), never darker with WEAR | – (at every setting) | in loop |
| Tape compression | slow 2:1, a little more loss above −18 dBFS (judged on the lower of a 1.5 s and a 50 ms level, v1.0.24) | × WEAR | in loop |
| Hiss and crackle | soft (3 kHz) hiss and sparse crackle into the tank's input, following the input level, so silence stays silent (ADR 0005) | × WEAR | tank input |
| Tape | record pre-emphasis (+6 dB above 2 kHz) around a clamped-cubic soft clip, up to 15 dB of drive taken off again, then the exact de-emphasis; a little bias for even harmonics. 48 kHz, no oversampling | drive with WEAR | wet |
| Breath | the BBD's NE570 compander, 2:1 in and 1:2 out, its noise between and the chip's soft 2nd harmonic; no line filters, so the noise and level breathe with the playing | with WEAR | wet |
| Clock | the BBD's sample-and-hold at its clock, 32 → 14 kHz (never lower: unfiltered it turns metallic), no anti-alias or reconstruction filter: the top folds back | blend × WEAR | wet |
| Grain | a step that follows the level, 9 → 5 bits under it (a floating step, like μ-law's), with a floor so silence stays silent: the grain stays the same share of a fading tail | with WEAR | wet |
| Band | the AM radio's gentle two-pole band-pass (~300 Hz-3 kHz, soft at the top), its over-modulation, and its wandering dip (a slow random notch, like a phaser); no station fades, detune or static. Whole at infinite DECAY too (v1.0.61; halved there v1.0.42-v1.0.60). Its over-modulation has a soft knee (linear to −0.5 of the carrier, then curving towards −1.25, v1.0.60). **MOTION's, not WEAR's, since v1.0.60** (ADR 0036) | blend × side^1.5 of MOTION's right side (`map::radioBand`) | wet |

- **Loudness:** a slow level hold on the wet chain (its 0.1 s levels above ~200 Hz compared, its gain gliding over ~0.5 s, within −6 / +12 dB) keeps music within ±1 dB of WEAR 0, and a small trim (0.7 dB × its blend) does the same for MOTION's band (LUFS; HALL and MASSIVE; `test_era`). Turned to 0 the chain runs on until the hold is back at unity, so it never clicks off.
- **Order on the wet:** after the tank, the twin and MOTION's transport; before TONE's tilt and bell (§4.1).
- **Removed in v1.0.42:** the Midiverb box (its 48 → 24 → 48 kHz halfbands and 13 bits in the loop), the bucket-brigade's and radio's own stages, tape out (`dsp/Tape.h`) and the cassette's wow and flutter.

### 4.8 (removed) Desk
Removed before the freeze, after the owner's demo listening: the knob became TIME (ADR 0015), shown as DEPTH since v1.0.42.

### 4.9 (removed v1.0.61) Ducking
Until v1.0.60 a 2:1 compressor keyed by the input held the wet down at infinite DECAY while you played (ADR 0014). Since v1.0.61 endless follows the playing (§4.4, ADR 0008 amendment): played, it is the longest finite tail, so there's no build-up to hold back, and it is gone.

### 4.10 Output stage
Tank + twin → MOTION's tape transport (in only on MOTION's left side; at rest the wet passes bit for bit) → WEAR's chain (§4.7) → TONE's tilt and bell (§3 P3) → soft limiter (ceiling −1 dBFS, Resilio-style) → BLEND. The dry path is untouched.

### 4.11 ONSET and DEPTH (ADR 0015, 0024; were ATTACK and TIME, ADR 0029)
- **NATURAL** (was NORMAL): DEPTH is predelay, 0 → 250 ms *(start)*. Massive adds its gentle built-in mass (§4.5) and Size's predelay.
- **BLOOM:** the swell stage (§4.5) is set so the tail's envelope rises over DEPTH, 0.12 → 4 s, in every space (× m(Size) in Massive). A short DEPTH keeps a swell from clashing with the harmony coming in (owner's demo note).
- **SHIMMER (v1.0.33, ADR 0024; replaces REVERSE, v1.0.9-v1.0.32):** the diffused input, shifted an octave up, is sent into the tank with it; no feedback, so one octave, steady and in tune. The shifter: a delay line read by 8 heads, each sweeping a 0.4 s grain at twice the speed of time, faded by sin² of its place in it, starting each grain at a random extra delay (up to a grain), so no note's octave is combed away; an 8th-order low-pass at 6 kHz before the shift, so nothing folds back above 24 kHz; a 9 kHz one-pole and an 80 Hz high-pass after. DEPTH sets the send (0 → 1.2 × the input). The same in every SPACE. While frozen, SHIMMER rests (its send fades out over 50 ms); it keeps recording, so switching in finds the last moments ready, and its recording fades with a SPACE flip as the tank's input does.

## 5. Performance budget

48 kHz, block 48 *(start)*. 480 MHz ÷ 48 kHz ≈ **10,000 cycles/sample**. Target **≤ 75 % peak** worst case, **80 % ceiling** for a release that passes the click check by ear (Resilio's numbers, ADR 0030).

### 5.3 Flash budget (128 KB internal, the tightest limit)
Resilio ended at 97.7 %. Sublustris tracks flash per milestone: **release ≤ 110 KB at v1.0** (≥ 18 KB free). From M0 it uses Resilio's techniques: no QSPI chip setup, no USB device stack in release, `-Os` on control-rate code (`IV_SIZE_OPT`), only shipped voicings compiled in, a size gate in every build. Two engines, not three (ADR 0006). **As built:** release 110,732 B at v1.0.47, 1,908 B under the 110 KB budget (112,640 B). The fallback if the budget can't be met, BOOT_SRAM with a custom linker script, loses the Firmware Swap install, so it is the **owner's call via a new ADR** (ADR 0002).

## 9. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Flash over 128 KB** (Resilio hit 97.7 %) | budget per milestone; two engines; Resilio's size techniques from M0; BOOT_SRAM only by owner ADR |
| SDRAM read cost under modulation | microbenchmark at M3, before features stack; swell stage and lines can trade length for speed |
