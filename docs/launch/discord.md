## Message

```
Hi all! Sublustris Versio, Resilio's sibling, is out today. It's free alternative firmware that turns the Versio into a big, dark reverb for ambient, dub techno and dark ambient. The same sound also runs as an AU/VST3 plugin on macOS, so you can try it without reflashing.

https://sublustris-versio.vercel.app/

What it does

* Past about 3 o'clock DECAY goes endless, follows your playing without a jump, and holds the chord when you stop, its notes still notes in PLATE and HALL
* Tap the button or send a gate to freeze the tail into a pad, and hold the button while frozen to paint what you play into it
* SPACE picks a dense PLATE, a big HALL or CANYON, a space with no roof that swells in with each sound
* ONSET brings each sound in at once (NATURAL), swells it in (BLOOM) or adds one steady octave on top (SHIMMER)
* MOTION is a tired cassette deck left of noon and an old AM radio right of it, and WEAR ages the tail with tape, bucket-brigade breathing, grit and hiss. Your dry signal is never touched

Links

* Download: https://sublustris-versio.vercel.app/#download
* Install guide: https://sublustris-versio.vercel.app/install/
* Manual: https://sublustris-versio.vercel.app/manual/
* Source: https://github.com/jeffebauer/sublustris-versio

I'd love your feedback. It's the first public release and I want to keep shaping it with the people playing it. If a tail sounds off, a knob feels dead, or there's a sound you want it to make, reply here or open an issue: https://github.com/jeffebauer/sublustris-versio/issues/new/choose. Ideas that make it in get credited in the changelog.

It's an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Noise Engineering. Thanks to NE for keeping the Versio open!
```
