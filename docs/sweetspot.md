Generated by `tools/sweetspot.py`: every knob in every SPACE, end to end in 10 steps. A step's size is its biggest change in units of roughly just-audible (1 dB loudness, 10 % decay, 1 semitone brightness, 15 % arrival or peak time, 0.1 width). **Dead zone:** 20 % or more of the travel where each step is under 0.25 of that. **Cliff:** one step over 3 and over 3× the knob's median step. DRIFT's pitch movement isn't measured. Decay is read from the click within 11 s, so DECAY's top (infinite) shows as one big step by design.
