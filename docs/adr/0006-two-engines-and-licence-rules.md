# 0006 — Two engines: a Plate (also the companion) and one FDN; licence rules

**Status:** Accepted, 21 Oct 2026 (owner). Spec-session decision 22, plus the [VERIFY] licence results.

**Context:** SPACE offers Plate / Hall / Massive. Flash is the tightest limit (ADR 0002). The brief says a Dattorro tank alone is not a differentiator (Plateau Versio, Vongon Ultrasheer): the FDN Massive mode and the character chain are.

**Decision:**
- **Plate:** a Dattorro-family figure-8 plate, written from Dattorro's 1997 paper. **The same code at a small size is the freeze companion** (scale ≤ 0.6, in DTCM).
- **Hall and Massive:** **one 8-line FDN, voiced two ways** (Hall: normal attack, big room; Massive: mass, long delays, echoes → wash, ADR 0007). 16 lines is a stretch goal, decided at M3.
- What keeps the Plate ours rather than Plateau's: every delay modulated, and the full character chain in the loop.
- **Licence rules:**
  - No code from Plateau Versio (GPL-3.0-or-later, from ValleyRackFree): concept only.
  - No code from the Daisy Griesinger reverb (no licence = all rights reserved; and it is Schroeder-style anyway).
  - Midiverb character only (ADR 0004).
  - MIT sources (e.g. DaisyCloudSeed, Qverb) may be read and credited.

**Rejected:**
- **One FDN for all three spaces:** least code, but a less classic plate.
- **Three separate engines:** most distinct, most flash and tuning work.
