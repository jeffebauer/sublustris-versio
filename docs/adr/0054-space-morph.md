# 0054 — The SPACE morph: a change keeps the tail

**Status:** Accepted, 9 Nov 2026 (owner: `renders/space_flip` "Morph 3s wins overall"; `renders/space_morph` C; `renders/space_morph2` S; `renders/morph_last_98` C on every card). SPEC v1.0.78. Amends ADR 0013's "only one big tank ever runs". The owner hasn't heard the port (only the pages, to the bit), and the port's own lap on the module is still to run.

**Context:**
- **The ask:** "prevent abrupt stops when changing between PLATE/HALL/MASSIVE". A change faded the wet out, restarted the tank and faded back in: everything ringing was lost.
- **Why ADR 0013 ruled it out:** two big tanks cost CPU and maybe CANYON's size. The freeze's spare tank went in v1.0.43, so HALL and CANYON, which share the FDN, can't run as two tanks. PLATE with HALL or CANYON can, for a few seconds.
- **The flash:** about 4.3 KB of morph against 468 B free. Paid for by building STM32 HAL's set-up files for size (`HAL_OS`, -5.2 KB release) and leaving out initialisers `prepare()` sets anyway (`IV_RI`, -360 B). No audio runs in those.

**Decision:**
- **PLATE <-> HALL or CANYON: a morph over 3 s.** Two tanks, both hearing the music, equal power on a raised cosine. The old tank keeps its own held settings and its own make-ups.
- **HALL <-> CANYON: a reshape of the one tank over 1.5 s (S).** Voicing ramps; the lines crossfade in SIZE's four staggered groups of 0.3 s, a quarter of them reading twice; the in-loop diffusion joins or leaves line by line, its first returns eased in; the early taps run both layouts at equal power. The one-trip correction takes DECAY's rate at the first group, since the sound in the line was left at the old rate.
- **The input still dips** over 0.05 s each way, as the flip always did, so its jumps stay unheard. The tank's sound doesn't dip.
- **Finish, then go** (owner, 9 Nov): while a morph or reshape runs the switch isn't read. When it ends the switch's latest position decides: a different space starts a normal new change, the same one does nothing. A there-and-back takes up to about 6 s.
- **C: half the shimmer's heads rest during a change into CANYON with SHIMMER on** (4 a side, not 8). A resting head finishes its grain and doesn't start another; it comes back at a grain's start. The level follows the running windows' power. It starts two control ticks (48 samples) after the handover, the module's heaviest block, and has its own loop (`Shimmer::headsLight`, in ITCM), so nothing else pays for it.
- **A freeze during a morph** keeps the sound you hear: the tank holding the most of it becomes the pad, the other goes in 0.3 s. A change while frozen waits for the unfreeze, as before.
- **A held endless tail dies away on a change to or from PLATE** (owner's call): the new tank only hears what's played next. HALL <-> CANYON carries it on.
- **Always on, every Host.** The prototype's hooks (`IV_SPACE_MORPH`, `IV_MORPH_LIGHT`, the log, the A/B/C/S switch, `SPACE_MORPH` as a measuring flag) are gone. Only a small event log stays, behind `#ifndef IV_FIXED_VOICINGS`, for `test_space_morph_waits`: no firmware bytes.

**Rejected:**
- **B on `renders/space_morph`, the old tank ringing out unheard of the music:** the owner's pick was the morph.
- **C on `renders/space_morph2`, the eight lines all reading twice for 1.2 s when the diffusion joins or leaves:** S spreads that load.
- **B on `renders/morph_last_98`, fewer swell points during the change:** the owner chose C by ear.
- **A second change on top of a running one:** 105.9 %, 966 late blocks (`docs/research/m3-morph-s2-lap.log`).

**Consequences:**
- **Module CPU, one lap of the pick (`docs/research/m3-morph-s6-lap.log`, on `main`):** 19 lines, 0 late blocks, worst 98.2 %. That's **not under the 95 % target**; the module starts dropping audio near 99 %, and a line can vary by up to 1.5 points between laps. The history: 105.9 % with stacked changes; 99.4 % with finish-then-go; 98.2 % with a new tank's allpasses on the fast path sooner (`Fdn::lineFast`); 99.5 % with C in the handover's block; 98.2 % with C just after it. `PLATE->CANYON SHIMMER` 98.0 % at 107 ms, `PLATE->CANYON->PLATE` 97.7 % at 50 ms, `CANYON->PLATE->CANYON` 98.2 % at 3156 ms, 156 ms after its second change.
- **Left undone:** spreading the handover's `apply` stage (about 4 points of its block, skipped: it would need a settings struct across about 440 lines, and bit-identity would be hard to keep); `CANYON->PLATE->CANYON` isn't helped by C (about 96.5-98.2 % with it, 96.2-96.8 % before).
- **SIZE's limit (v1.0.77, ADR 0053) stays in the reshape,** and it's the one place the port isn't the page's bits: the page had no limit. At the page's DECAY (0.6), and from 0.3 up, nothing moves. CANYON -> HALL at DECAY 0.15 or under differs (largest sample difference 0.31, DECAY 0.05, SIZE 1, a pad, against the limiter's -1 dBFS peak in both; 0.11 at DECAY 0.15; 10 of 60 cases at DECAY 0.05-0.6). HALL -> CANYON, a growth, never does.
- **Bit-identical to the pick, renderer against renderer at `d8f2058`:** the page's 12 changes and held CANYON control (14/14), and 102 more cases: all six pairs with NATURAL, BLOOM and SHIMMER on three materials, re-flips, a change while frozen, freezes early and late in a change.
- **`same_render` against v1.0.77 (`f4814a7`):** SAME 59 of 60. `flips`, the one with SPACE changes, differs from the first change on.
- **Clicks (1 ms frames above 5 kHz, over the median of the second around them, 125 cases, 259 moments: every pair, re-flips, a change while frozen, freezes during a change, endless DECAY, SIZE, TIME and DECAY swept through it):** all finite. Plain changes peak at -51.3 dBFS above 5 kHz (v1.0.77's own flips -53.2): a soft wash from the groups' fades, 45 dB or more under the signal. The worst moment is a freeze in the first half-second of HALL -> PLATE on a sparse tune with SHIMMER on: about -59 dBFS above 5 kHz for 0.1 s, about 30 dB under the full band, where the same freeze with no change is -83. It's in the pick, to the bit.
- **Energy:** 108 changes (input level 0.1, 0.5, 1.0; every pair; DECAY 0.6, 0.85, endless; NATURAL and SHIMMER), all finite: 1 s RMS at most +0.55 dB over the louder of the two steady spaces, peaks at the limiter's 0.891 where the steady ones are.
- **Endless holds through a change, 600 s of silence after:** 12 cases, fed and held. Fed: 0.07-0.12 dB drift. Held: HALL <-> CANYON stays within 0.28 dB; to or from PLATE goes to silence.
- **Tests:** `test_control_latch` now asserts that a switch left away past the fade's bottom commits to the change, keeps the tail (within 6 dB of no change), and goes back when the switch says so once it's finished; it asserted the tail was wiped. `test_space_morph_waits` is new. Goldens unchanged, none updated: they hold settled settings.
- **Flash, clean rebuild:** release 111,164 B (1,476 B under the 112,640 budget), profile 130,580 B (492 B under the chip), m0test 83,556 B; ITCM 62,848 B of 64,512 (release), 62,688 B (profile). The profile build drops six MICRO tests (VDIV and swell), the headroom steps and the size_moves hunt to fit the morph's lap (`RunMorphLap`, 19 lines).
- **Not checked:** the port on the module (the lap above is the prototype's; the port adds v1.0.77's limit and the restart check); the owner's ears on the port; a change at DECAY under 0.3 by ear; the desktop's unexplained difference after `Engine::reset()` depending on what ran before (seen on the prototype, not looked at).
