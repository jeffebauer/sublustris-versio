# 0008 — Infinite tails evolve to a warm floor

**Status:** Accepted, 21 Oct 2026 (owner). Spec-session decision 8.

**Context:** Valhalla's "Infinite Supermassive" post keeps EQ outside the loop and sets mod depth to 0, so a frozen tail keeps its pitch and brightness. The brief puts damping, saturation and ERA inside the loop, so the tail darkens and grains as it decays. The brief also wants tails that "sustain near-infinitely without running away".

**Decision:**
- At DECAY 0.85–1 the loop gain reaches 1 at the floor. TONE, ERA and the saturation **stay in the loop**, so the tail keeps slowly darkening and graining.
- The always-on soft saturator plus a slow RMS loop-level governor hold it at a **warm floor** (−24 dBFS RMS, a starting guess). It never turns to mud and never falls silent.
- Test: a 10-minute render at DECAY 1 holds the floor (±3 dB, no growth, no NaN) at every ERA stage.
- The frozen pad is different: it holds still (ADR 0009).

**Rejected:** holding still at infinite (in-loop colour bypassed); Andromeda-style fade over thousands of seconds instead of true infinite.

**Amendment (21 Oct 2026, M5/M6 build; SPEC v1.0.2 and v1.0.3; the makeup is for the owner to approve):**
- **Held tails hold.** Once the input has been quiet for 2 s, an infinite tail's own level (on an 8 s average, settled over 10 s) becomes its ceiling, so it can't creep up toward the floor. A frozen pad's ceiling is its own level 20 s after the freeze (ADR 0009). Above the ceiling the governor adds loss in proportion, as at the floor.
- **Makeup (an exception to SPEC §2.3 rule 2, loop gain ≤ 1):** a held tail that sags below its ceiling is raised back toward it, never above it, at most ~0.3 dB/s. Moving allpass-interpolated reads (fast DRIFT, the cassette's wow and flutter) lose a little energy, and without makeup an infinite tail at the cassette stage faded ~0.1 dB/s (60 dB in 10 minutes). The exception is bounded: only a held tail, only up to its own level, slowly.
- **Measured:** infinite tails hold within 0.5 dB for 10 minutes at every ERA stage (`test_era`), pads within 1 dB for 5 minutes in every SPACE (`test_plate_freeze`), and the levels stay put across random seeds.
- **Rejected:** a one-sided or symmetric-capped correction (it overshot into dips of several dB while Neve's even harmonics were still in the loop at infinite); keeping Neve's even harmonics at infinite (they made the tail jump 2-3 dB by themselves).

**Approved (23 Oct 2026, owner's pick on `renders/makeup`, A on all 4 cards; SPEC v1.0.17):** the held-tail makeup stays. Measured then: without it an infinite MASSIVE with tape wobble and the cassette sagged about 3 dB over 10 minutes and a HALL about 2.4 dB; frozen pads held either way.

**Amendment (24 Oct 2026, owner's OK; SPEC v1.0.24):** the governor's loss, and ERA's cassette compression, judge a falling tail fast. Each takes the lower of its 1.5 s level and a 50 ms level, so a tail that drops when the input stops is never judged as still loud. A held tail's hold level is taken only once the tank's own input is quiet (in BLOOM the swell keeps feeding the tank after the input stops). Before this, a loud input stopping made an infinite tail plunge (PLATE −27 dB, fed 12 dB hot) and creep back over minutes. Now every dip is within ~2 dB. Found by the overnight soak (`docs/research/soak-audit.md`).

**Amendment (29 Oct 2026, owner's pick B on every card of `renders/review2_bump`; SPEC v1.0.56):** **in PLATE and MASSIVE the governor eases in from DECAY 0.70**, its weight t³ over 0.70-0.85 (`voicing::kGovernorEaseFrom`, `map::floorWeight`); HALL's comes in across the 0.80-0.85 crossover as before.
- **Why:** the owner heard MASSIVE ~4 dB louder just before endless than at endless while playing, and a drop at about 3 o'clock on a slow turn. Measured (overnight review 2, `docs/research/review2-notes/bump.md`): at a fifth of its weight the governor already out-loses a 120 s tail, so ~75 % of the change sat between 0.80 and 0.81 (2.5-4.6 dB per 0.01 of DECAY in MASSIVE, 1.0-2.3 dB in PLATE). Its size depends on the playing level (endless is level-governed, finite settings are linear), so no fixed gain could fix it; the cliff could go.
- **PLATE's long-tail trim (ADR 0034) lets go on the same curve** ((1 − the governor's weight)³). With the governor alone eased, the trim's release stepped PLATE back up 0.7-0.9 dB at 0.80 → 0.81.
- **Unchanged:** the duck, the hold, the darkening and everything else DECAY's crossover fades keep the crossover's own weight; every DECAY at or below 0.70 and from 0.85 is bit-identical, as is HALL. The governor only adds loss in this range (the hold's make-up needs an endless or frozen tail), so nothing new can grow.
- **Rejected (the review's prototypes):** a fixed make-up trim before endless (it flipped the step's direction for quiet playing and stabs); the ramp inside 0.80-0.85 only (still a 1.9 dB step, and 0.74-0.80 as loud); easing the duck as well (within 0.1-0.6 dB of the governor alone, 8 B more flash).

**Amendment (29 Oct 2026, owner's picks on `renders/review2_endless_final`, after F with E's tone on `renders/review2_endless2` and B on `renders/review2_endless`; SPEC v1.0.58):** **a held endless tail keeps still notes; only a bright one settles a little; the governor's correction is a flat gain.**
- **Why:** the overnight review 2 (`docs/research/review-2026-10-29.md`, "The rumble at endless DECAY"; `docs/research/review2-notes/rumble.md`, `endless.md`, `endless2.md`) found that the governor's correction was spectral. Any loss from it switched the air (v1.0.25) on in full, and its make-up lifted the lows alone, so while it dithered round a held tail's level the tail drained its highs (PLATE, TONE fully clockwise: 40+ dB at 4 kHz by 5 minutes) and piled up its lows (+9 dB under 200 Hz over 10 minutes), with the total held. A pad drained too, and DC coming in at endless stayed. With that fixed (B), the owner heard the held tail's top end as "too much like noise": the loop's modulation smears held notes into a haze, and at endless nothing lets the smear go (with the modulation off the tail stays clean notes for 10 minutes). They picked F ("still notes") with E's tone ("settles light"); hearing the two together they kept still notes alone on every soft-chord card and the settle only on a bright one (a noise swell over the chord): "still notes everywhere, with a light settle only when the held tail is bright".
- **Decision:**
  - The governor's correction (the floor's loss, the hold's loss and make-up) is a **flat gain**, the same at the highs as at the lows; TONE's ratio, the air and the infinite fade shape DECAY's own loss only (`Governor.h absorbShared`'s `flat`).
  - **The still lows** take their leader's floor loss but not the hold's correction (it lifted them with the make-up meant for the modulated main tank; lossless at endless, they hold their level as they are).
  - **The wet's input loses its DC** below 5 Hz (`voicing::kInputDcHz`, one pole, outside the loop): −0.2 dB at 20-40 Hz.
  - **A held endless tail** (the input quiet for 2 s at full endless DECAY), PLATE and HALL, not frozen: the modulation FREEZE glides out glides out of it too over 10 s (`kEndlessCalmSeconds`; new playing brings it back over 1 s, as an unfreeze), and its highs lose at least a rate that fades with `kEndlessTopSeconds` (60 s), so its top end mellows by up to about `kEndlessTopDb` (24 dB) above the loss shelf at as built's early pace and stops (`absorbShared`'s `top`), **in proportion to how bright the playing was**: its mean frequency by its slope (sqrt of the sample-to-sample difference's power over its power, × fs / 2π, every 4th input sample), followed over 1 s while the input is above the quiet level and held while it's quiet (`Engine::trackBrightness`); none up to 1.2 kHz, all from 6 kHz (`kEndlessBright*`). A held tail is fed nothing, so its share holds (no feedback from the settle itself, no flipping); while playing it glides with the follower. Measured: the pad chord 449 Hz, keys and stabs ≤ 471 Hz (none, bit-identical to still notes alone), a saw chord 1.4 kHz (0.04), hats over the chord 3.8 kHz (0.55), a cymbal or noise swell 9.9-10.6 kHz (all). Where TONE's or the infinite fade's own high loss is larger (TONE noon and below) that one stands. MASSIVE keeps its slow wash and ADR 0022's darkening.
- **Why as built "sounded like a more resonant filter" (the owner, on A):** the air switched on by the hold's nudges, applied pass after pass, is a very steep low-pass that keeps closing (PLATE TONE fully clockwise at 5 minutes, 1/3-octaves under the strongest: 1 / 1.6 / 2 kHz at −20 / −43 / −56 dB, about −40 dB an octave; F + E −10 / −19 / −25; by 10 minutes 1 kHz −34), so the chord's highest surviving partials ring out at its edge; PLATE's tail ends up as a handful of low partials (half its energy in 7 one-hertz bins at 10 minutes; F + E 16). Control: as built with only the air taken out at endless measures as B to within ~1 dB in every third-octave; the low-shelf make-up and the 2.5 kHz shelf alone don't do it.
- **Unchanged:** every finite DECAY (but for the input's DC, and PLATE and MASSIVE between 0.70 and 0.85 while the floor governor eases in, where its loss is now flat too); the floor, the hold's ceilings, and a fed endless tail's level (within 0.5 dB).
- **Open:** on bright material the settle's darker top is made up by the hold's flat gain, so the low end comes up underneath (the noise swell over the chord: under 500 Hz +7 dB at 5 minutes against still notes alone); the owner heard it and picked it there. ADR 0008's never-ending darkening at TONE noon is untouched (the review's next idea: a hold that makes up only what the modulation loses).
- **Rejected (the pages):** as built; B alone (the haze); deeper settles (C 72 dB, D 48 dB: too dark); E alone (the haze sinks but stays); still notes with the settle on every tail (`renders/review2_endless_final` C: the soft chord cards went to still notes alone).

**Amendment (2 Nov 2026, owner's pick C, "endless follows the playing", on all 14 cards of `renders/decay_endless_match`; SPEC v1.0.61):** **while you play, endless is the longest finite tail; once you stop, it holds.** ADR 0014's duck is gone.
- **Why:** the owner, on v1.0.60: "I'm hearing level shifts between endless and non-endless transition points at 100% wet... the transition between them should be imperceptible from an amplitude perspective". `renders/decay_blend` showed the step was in the reverb, not the blend, and that it flipped with the input: endless was governed to one floor (about −18 dBFS in HALL), so a hot tail was pulled down to it and a quiet one pushed up (into endless, HALL: up to 6.4 dB). No established unit governs infinite to a fixed dBFS floor: they run the feedback at 1 and let a limiter or saturation bound it (Eventide, Valhalla), or freeze with the input muted (BigSky, Clouds) (`docs/research/endless-match.md`). This keeps the finite top's own loss while played and freezes, with the input open, once it's quiet.
- **Decision:**
  - **Played:** past DECAY 0.80 the tail keeps DECAY 0.80's loss, level make-up, long-tail trims (ADR 0034), MASSIVE's high ratio and its governor at the floor (PLATE and MASSIVE ease it in from 0.70, the v1.0.56 amendment). Crossing 3 o'clock can't step the level, quiet or hot, and a hot input is bounded exactly as the longest finite tail is.
  - **The hold:** once the tank's input is 30 dB under the tail for 0.25 s (`voicing::kEndlessQuietBelowDb`, `kEndlessQuietSeconds`; relative, so a long BLOOM still feeding the tank faintly doesn't leave the tail fading first), its loss glides out over 0.5 s (`kEndlessHoldSeconds`) and the tail holds where it is. About 0.75 s in all. The governor's floor lets go within 50 ms of the input going quiet (`kEndlessFloorSeconds`: held until the hold, it pulled a fed PLATE tail down 8 dB) and glides to a **−6 dBFS safety ceiling** (`kEndlessSafetyDbfs`) at full weight; the hold's own ceiling (the tail's settled level, the 21 Oct amendment, resynced to its fast level when the 2 s quiet comes) stops any creep. New playing lets go at once. Across the 0.80-0.85 crossover only the hold comes in (by the crossover's weight).
  - Still notes and the light settle (the v1.0.58 amendment) start with the hold, as well as after the 2 s quiet.
  - **No duck (ADR 0014 superseded):** with endless at the long tail's level there's no build-up to hold back, and kept on its own the duck stepped the level 1.2-1.9 dB played hot.
- **Measured (the page, 100 % wet, the mean 0.25-0.75 s after each turn against the same render with DECAY held; worst of six sources quiet and hot):** into / out / into on the tail / out on the tail, HALL 0.2 / 0.1 / 0.6 / 0.5 dB, PLATE 0.6 / 0.3 / 1.9 / 0.7 (the tail-in figure is the hold stopping PLATE's fade, not a jump), MASSIVE 1.1 / 0.9 / 0.7 / 0.7 (v1.0.60: 6.4 / 1.6 / 2.6 / 1.6, 2.9 / 1.2 / 2.5 / 1.0, 4.9 / 2.6 / 1.9 / 0.4). Fed hot for 2 minutes endless sits at 0.80's level (PLATE and MASSIVE exactly, HALL +0.3 dB), peaks −2.6 dBFS or under. Held, 10 minutes: within 0.5 dB on noise from 10 s on (PLATE, HALL, MASSIVE); a chord within about 1.3 dB (PLATE's hold make-up bringing it back up to its ceiling in the first minute, HALL's beating ±1 dB), MASSIVE's wash wandering as before. No growth in silence. PLATE still loses about 7 dB in the first second after stopping: the early sound leaving, the same at 0.80; then it holds.
- **Unchanged:** every finite DECAY (to 0.80) bit for bit; FREEZE (ADR 0009); the hold's ceilings and make-up.
- **Changed with it:** at endless DECAY the twin's level follows the 0.80 tail too, so HALL's room played over a freeze is 2.1 dB quieter there (its long-tail trim), and PLATE's twin sits 1.4 dB closer to its pad (the pad isn't ducked any more).
- **Rejected (the page):** A, v1.0.60; B, v1.0.60 with DECAY crossing the 0.80-0.85 crossover in 3 s (`renders/decay_cross`'s pick, not built: the owner picked C over D, the same with the 3 s crossing); D. Kept the duck (value 2 of the prototype hook): its own 1.2-1.9 dB steps played hot.
