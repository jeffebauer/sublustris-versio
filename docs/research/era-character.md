# ERA: what each era sounds like, and where it overlaps TONE and DRIFT

26 Oct 2026, for the owner's ERA rethink (session 9). Sources: SPEC §4.6-4.7, `core/params/EraVoicing.h`, ADR 0004 / 0021, round 15 (`docs/research/m3-round15.log` on `claude/massive-air`). As built in v1.0.40.

**Two kinds of darkening.** *In the loop* (TONE, the Neve, the Midiverb's dark loop, the cassette's highs): every pass round the tank loses a little top, so a tail starts bright and **darkens as it fades**. *On the output* (the BBD's and the radio's filters): a fixed curtain over the whole wet, so the tail is **dark from the first reflection** and stays the same colour as it fades.

| Era (knob) | What you hear | How | Overlaps TONE | Overlaps DRIFT | CPU (round 15, over the Neve) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Neve** (0-20 %, under all) | warm, slightly rounded top; the base | highs decay ×0.7 faster in the loop; gentle even harmonics into the soft clip | yes, mildly: the same in-loop darkening TONE does | no | – |
| **Midiverb** (20-40 %) | a dark, slightly grainy late-80s digital tail; quiet tails end a little sooner; a rare crackle | in the loop: a ½ one-pole low-pass every pass (darkens fast), 13-bit truncation (fine grain, fades end sooner), crackle into the input | **strongly**: its dark loop is the same kind of darkening as TONE turned down; its ~10 kHz ceiling now matches the 24 kHz tank's own top (v1.0.35), so it adds little there | no | +2.7 % PLATE, +7 % MASSIVE |
| **Bucket-brigade** (40-60 %) | muffled, warm, "through a box"; noise and level breathe with your playing; a gritty, slightly unpitched top | on the wet: low-pass 7 → 2.2 kHz across its stretch, weak anti-alias (aliasing), NE570 2:1 compander (the breathing), a growing 2nd harmonic | **partly**: the result is dark like TONE down, but it's a fixed curtain (dark from the start), not a tail that darkens; the breathing and aliasing are its own | no (no clock wobble, unlike a BBD chorus) | +5.3 % PLATE, +6.2 % MASSIVE |
| **Cassette** (60-80 %) | saturated, dense, rounded; highs soften as they get louder; faint hiss | tape drive in the loop, tape out on the wet with pre-emphasis (the top saturates first), 2:1 compression above −18 dBFS, hiss into the input, highs ×0.4 in the loop | **yes**: its ×0.4 highs is TONE's kind of darkening, stronger | **yes, a little**: wow ±4 c at 0.55 Hz and flutter ±1 c at 7 Hz are added to DRIFT's left side (DRIFT reaches ±15 c) | +9.7 % PLATE, +11.4 % MASSIVE (the most) |
| **AM radio** (80-100 %) | thin, distant, a station fading in and out; a wandering dip like a slow phaser; crackle, lightning, hum | on the wet: band-pass (200-400 Hz to 4.5-2.4 kHz), a wandering notch, over-modulation, the station's fade with an AGC, a slow detune | only at the top (it cuts highs too); the low cut and the moving dip are its own | **a little**: the slow detune | +6.1 % PLATE, +7 % MASSIVE |

**What only one era does** (worth keeping in any rethink): the Midiverb's 13-bit grain; the BBD's breathing compander and aliasing; the cassette's saturation and compression; the radio's band-pass, moving dip and fading. **What several eras and TONE all do:** take the highs off the tail (Neve, Midiverb, cassette in the loop; BBD, radio on the output).
