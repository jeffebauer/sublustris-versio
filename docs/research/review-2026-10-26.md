## 1. Method and baseline

**The question.** The module (a Cortex-M7 at 480 MHz, 48 kHz, 48-sample blocks) gives the Engine 10,000 cycles for every stereo sample. The owner's headroom test (round 9b) showed blocks stay clean up to 96 % of that and start arriving late at 101 %, so the real target is **about 90 % at the worst block**. After v1.0.35 MASSIVE with BLOOM still averaged 115-129 % and peaked at 127 %; HALL 101-123 %; PLATE fits (59-69 %, ~88 % peak). v1.0.36 (fewer early reflections, the prime table) has not been measured yet.

**What was done tonight.**
- Everything here was read from the code, the ARM disassembly of the release firmware (`arm-none-eabi-objdump -d` of `firmware/build/immensio_versio.elf`), the ten rounds of measurements in `docs/TASKS.md`, and fetched sources. Nothing was flashed or installed.
- Seven background agents did the wide reading (three on the literature, three on per-area cost models, one on architecture and tests); every claim taken from them was re-checked against the code or the disassembly before it went in here. Where it could not be, it says so.
- Prototypes were built on this branch, each behind a renderer hook, and compared **sample for sample** against the baseline renderer with a new committed tool, `tools/same_render.py` (55 float renders: every SPACE; SIZE, ERA, DECAY, DRIFT and ATTACK corners; SIZE and TIME moves and flicks; SPACE flips; freezes and paints; an ERA sweep; an odd block size). "Sound-neutral" below means that tool printed `SAME 55/55`.

**The baseline on this branch** (`0bf5505`, before any change of this review):
- Tests: `100% tests passed out of 14` (`build-review/ctest.log`; the stimulus WAVs must be generated first with `python3 tools/make_stimulus.py`, or `test_era` and `test_metrics` fail).
- Firmware: release **112,180 B** (460 B under the 112,640 B budget), profile 116,432 B, m0test 82,476 B.
- `tools/same_render.py` on the baseline renderer against itself: `SAME 55/55` (17 s on this Mac).

**How to read the numbers.** Cycle counts are per 48 kHz stereo sample unless said otherwise; "% of budget" is that over 10,000. The tank runs at 24 kHz, so one *tank sample* happens every other output sample: a tank cost of 3,000 cycles a tank sample is 1,500 a sample, 15 % of the budget.
