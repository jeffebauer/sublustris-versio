# Starting points

Here are eight settings to start from, with what to play into them and what to move next. From there, move by ear. The Versio has no preset memory, so you'll set the knobs by hand. In the plugin you can save them as presets in your DAW.

**Reading the panels.** Each starting point shows the panel with its knobs and switches set, and the exact values underneath. Knob values run from 0 (fully left, 7 o'clock) to 1 (fully right, 5 o'clock). Noon is 0.5, and each hour is about 0.1.

MOTION is bipolar. At noon it only has a faint movement, to the left it's tape, and to the right it's an old radio. DECAY turns endless past about 3 o'clock (0.8), fully from about 3:30 (0.85).

## Endless pad

**Sounds like:** each chord joining the last in a slow, warm wash that never ends.

**Play into it:** slow pad chords, a bar or two each, with gaps.

**Move next:** SIZE fully up for a boundless wash. TONE further left to sink it. Switch ONSET to BLOOM for chords that swell in.

## Dub techno hall

**Sounds like:** a big room behind offbeat chord stabs. The tail fills the gaps, steps back while the stabs play and blooms between them.

**Play into it:** chord stabs, as an insert. On a send, turn BLEND fully right on the return.

**Move next:** MOTION a little further left for tape wow. WEAR up for grit. Pull DECAY just under 3 o'clock for a long tail that fades.

## Drum plate

**Sounds like:** a short, dense plate that thickens a drum loop without washing it out.

**Play into it:** a drum loop or a mix bus.

**Move next:** DEPTH up for predelay, so the hits stay clear of the plate. SIZE up for a bigger plate. TONE right for a brighter splash.

## Freeze bed

**Sounds like:** a held chord, still as glass, with a quieter room over it for whatever you play.

**Play into it:** a chord, then tap the button. Play a melody over the pad. Hold the button through the phrase you want to keep.

**Move next:** SIZE sets the room you play over the pad. DECAY sets how long it rings. MOTION left puts tape wow on the pad itself.

## Bloom swell

**Sounds like:** each note swelling in behind itself over a second or two, then hanging.

**Play into it:** single piano notes, plucks or a guitar, with space between them.

**Move next:** DEPTH sets the swell time: shorter for faster harmony, longer for slow pieces.

## Shimmer bed

**Sounds like:** a slow melody with a soft octave above it, sinking into a long hall.

**Play into it:** a slow synth lead or a high piano line.

**Move next:** DEPTH for more or less octave. MOTION a little right for an old radio's haze over the octave. DECAY past 3:30 to hold it.

## Worn tape room

**Sounds like:** an old tape of a big room: warm, grainy, with wow and a little crinkle.

**Play into it:** electric piano, guitar or a vocal.

**Move next:** WEAR toward 5 o'clock for more grit. MOTION fully left for dropouts, or past noon to the right to trade the tape for an old radio's narrow, phasing band.

## Dark drone

**Sounds like:** a low, slow wash that hangs and slowly darkens, grainy at the edges.

**Play into it:** a low drone, a bass note or a field recording.

**Move next:** patch a slow LFO into SIZE. Tap a freeze and play over the drone.
