# Changelog

Newest first. There's no public release yet, so below is the story so far, in a few steps. From the first release on, each version gets its own entry, with the firmware for the Versio and the plugin for macOS.

When a player suggests a change, I credit them in its entry. If you have an idea, [suggest a sound](https://github.com/jeffebauer/sublustris-versio/issues/new?template=sound_idea.yml).

## In development

<p class="release-version">v1.0.68, 3 November 2026</p>

Almost every sound change below was made by ear: each option went onto a listening page next to the version before it, and I picked.

### 3 November: a plate that changes size cleanly, and more worn tape

- **PLATE's SIZE crossfades** to its new size, like HALL and CANYON, so turning it no longer bends the pitch.
- **A more worn cassette** on MOTION's left: deeper wow and flutter, and crinkle and dropouts from about 9:30, more often and deeper further left.
- **WEAR without the chip's clock.** The delay chip's clock, which folded the top end back, is gone; WEAR is tape, the chip's breathing and grain.
- **A lighter radio.** Only the loudest peaks flatten on MOTION's right, so fully right no longer sounds overdriven.
- **A centred onset.** The first reflections in HALL and CANYON no longer pull against each other, so they hold up in mono.
- **Sturdier controls.** A short pulse at the FREEZE jack is never missed, and brushing the SPACE switch no longer restarts the tail.
- **CANYON** is the new name for MASSIVE, SPACE's third position. Sets saved with MASSIVE open on it.

### 2 November: endless follows your playing

- **Smooth into and out of endless.** While you play, an endless tail sits at the level of the longest tail before it, so turning DECAY across 3 o'clock no longer jumps, quiet or hot. When you stop, it holds where it is. It no longer steps back while you play.
- **The radio stays the radio.** MOTION's right side keeps its full narrow band in endless too.

### 1 November: an old radio on MOTION's right

- **MOTION's right side is an old AM radio:** the tail narrows to the radio's band and a slow notch wanders through it, so it phases. It replaces the wander, which stayed too subtle.
- **WEAR is tape, bucket-brigade and bit wear** over the warm desk colour; the radio's band moved to MOTION, so the two can be set apart.

### 28–29 October: longer tails, and an endless hold that keeps its notes

- **Longer tails.** DECAY reaches about 30 seconds in PLATE, a minute in HALL and two in CANYON before it turns endless, and the knob spends more of its travel on long, lush tails.
- **Still notes.** Leave an endless PLATE or HALL alone and its movement settles, so held notes stay notes instead of smearing into haze. A bright wash also mellows slowly. Play again and it all comes back.
- **A long HALL keeps its upper mids** ringing nearer the body, so it stays open instead of going dull.
- **Even levels.** HALL and CANYON sit at the same loudness, endless tails sit a little higher, and turning DECAY no longer swells the level on the way.
- **Gentler ducking.** An endless tail steps back about 3 dB while you play, and held chords move it less than stabs.
- **Tape dropouts** on MOTION's left side come in earlier, from about 7:30.

### 25–27 October: the knobs find their jobs

- **WEAR** replaced a knob that ran through five eras of old gear in turn. It's one amount of tape, bucket-brigade, bit and radio wear over a warm desk colour, and it starts clean.
- **MOTION's left side is a tape transport:** the whole tail bends together, with wow, flutter, crinkle and dropouts. Its right side wanders wider.
- **TONE** got a soft, warm swell turned left and a sheen turned right.
- **The small room.** What you play over a freeze in HALL and CANYON now rings in a small, light room on top of the pad, about 8 dB under it.
- **SIZE** moves in HALL and CANYON crossfade instead of bending the pitch.
- **SHIMMER** replaced a reverse reverb on the ONSET switch: one steady octave, in tune.
- **CANYON's highs** ring as long as its body, and its shortest tail lasts longer.
- **New names.** The project became Sublustris Versio, and the controls got their panel names: BLEND, WEAR, MOTION, DEPTH and ONSET were MIX, ERA, DRIFT, TIME and ATTACK. Saved sets in the plugin still load.

### 21–24 October: the first sounds

- The three spaces, PLATE, HALL and CANYON, and the endless hold that settles instead of running away.
- **Freeze and paint**, with the button and the gate.
- **Still lows:** in HALL and CANYON the bass stays still while everything above it moves.
- HALL starts right behind the note, and DECAY keeps the loudness at any setting.
